## 3. Evidence architecture applied

PASS 4 preserves five evidence levels:

- **E0 — External scientific foundation:** peer-reviewed literature, established constructs, measurement precedents and methodological standards.
- **E1 — P-120 internal architecture verification:** controlled construct-boundary, ownership, redundancy, item-level, information-gain, scope/exposure, safety/privacy and cross-module development records.
- **E2 — Human/cognitive/cross-check evidence:** only evidence-bearing observations actually collected and adjudicated.
- **E3 — Psychometric validation:** respondent-sample quantitative validation.
- **E4 — External replication:** separately established replication evidence.

The critical firewall is unchanged: E0 and E1 are legitimate evidence classes, but neither is E3. A desk-stage cross-check, red-team PASS or controlled evidence-gathering protocol is not psychometric validation.
