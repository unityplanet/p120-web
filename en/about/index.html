P-120 · ABOUT THE SYSTEM

What P-120 is

P-120 is not one test and not one overall score. It is a governed research architecture in which independent measurement domains, computation, interpretation, validation and system development are connected — without becoming interchangeable.

SHORT DEFINITION

P-120 organises measurement, computation, interpretation, validation and governed development within one coherent system while keeping final scientific and governance responsibility human.

Research Candidate18+multidimensional profile
01
FROM TEST TO ARCHITECTURE

The questionnaire is an entry point, not the whole system

A respondent sees questions, navigation, saved progress, results and a report. Before any measurement object reaches that surface, P-120 separately defines its construct territory, its boundary from adjacent constructs, its permitted response mode, its treatment of unavailable experience, and the inferences that remain prohibited.

After an answer is collected, the chain remains layered. Deterministic scoring is used only where authorised; derived outputs remain traceable to source measurements; interpretation is governed separately; and the respondent report is a publication surface rather than a new source of scientific authority.

P-120 is not “a questionnaire plus software.” It is an architecture governing how evidence becomes measurement, and how measurement may become interpretation.

03
MULTI-LAYER TOPOLOGY

Independent coordinates first. Authorised integration second.

Individual profile

Source measurement layers are defined independently. No adjacent process is used as an automatic proxy for another.

→
Extended directions

A new module is justified only when it asks an independent research question. Its incremental value remains an empirical question.

→
Dyadic layer

Profile A and Profile B are measured independently first. Only then may a separate governed layer compare permitted source measurements.

P-120 does not authorise a universal “compatibility percentage” merely because two profiles can be compared. Similarity, discrepancy, complementarity and interaction are different analytical objects.

04
FROM RESPONSE TO RESULT

Each level must retain its own status

01

Measurement

A structured response inside a defined construct and administration mode.

02

Computation

Deterministic rules are applied only to objects for which they are formally authorised.

03

Derived output

A rule-based result remains traceable to source data and does not become a new independent fact.

04

Interpretation

Meaningful synthesis is limited by its own rules, evidence status and the observations actually available.

05

Publication

A report explains authorised results to a person, but literary language cannot increase scientific certainty.

05
SCIENTIFIC STATUS AND VALIDATION

Different forms of checking answer different questions

Internal verification

Scientific logic, construct boundaries, redundancy and cross-module consistency.

Empirical validation

Psychometric properties tested with respondent data: structure, reliability, distinctness and related properties.

Synthetic technical validation

Technical end-to-end tests of forms, persistence, calculations, reports, missing/N-A and edge cases using synthetic data.

Production QA

Implementation, regression, accessibility, rendering and release-specific conformance checks.

Independent audit

A separate external or independent critical-review layer that does not substitute for human data.

P-120 remains a Research Candidate. Substantial architectural development and internal verification are not the same as completed empirical psychometric validation.
06
GOVERNED DEVELOPMENT

The architecture must be able to change without losing the basis for its decisions

01
Detection

A problem is classified concretely: measurement, code, interpretation, privacy, publication or another controlled domain.

02
Authority

The relevant specialised authority decides within its domain. A narrative cannot replace a scientific or technical source.

03
Verification

The change passes the applicable gates and regression checks before receiving a new status.

04
Traceable release

A new version retains a clear chain from the reason for change to the resulting artefact and evidence.

05
Governed organisational memory

Decisions and their basis persist across documents, registries, code, databases and release history rather than depending on the memory of one model.

06
Second-order research architecture

P-120 governs not only research on its object but also how research claims are created, checked, revised and granted authoritative status.

QUALIFIED TERM

Self-governing research architecture means that P-120 contains explicit procedures governing its own development.

It does not mean autonomy. The system does not appoint itself as a source of scientific truth and does not acquire independent epistemic authority. Rules, precedence and final decisions remain under human responsibility.

Self-correction therefore means explicit routes for detection, verification, local correction and release — not automatic “self-learning” of scientific truth.

07
HUMAN AUTHORITY AND COMPUTATIONAL ENVIRONMENT

More computational capacity does not mean more scientific authority

P-120 is developed with extensive use of modern computational capabilities and language-model systems. Architecturally, these are treated as a Founder-governed computational research environment.

Such an environment can substantially increase working volume, automation, comparison capacity, version coordination and the number of dependencies handled at once. It does not become an independent source of scientific truth, problem formation or final judgement.

No fixed quantitative productivity multiplier is claimed.

08
BOUNDARIES

What P-120 does not claim

Not a diagnosis

Structured self-report does not reveal a hidden essence of a person and does not become a clinical diagnosis.

Not objective truth about motives

Self-report can make experience more observable and comparable, but it does not provide direct access to unconscious causes.

Not one universal score

Related human processes are not collapsed into a single number merely because they can be compared.

Not self-validation

Governance quality, code quality or internal verification does not substitute for psychometric validation with human data.

P-120 · SYSTEM DEFINITION

P-120 is a governed research architecture for studying structured patterns of adult erotic, embodied and relational experience. It keeps measurement, computation, interpretation, validation and governance distinct — and connects them only through explicit, traceable rules.
