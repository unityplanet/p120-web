P-120 · EXTENDED RESEARCH SYSTEM

Want to go deeper?

Optional Deep-Dive Research Layers

P-120 already provides an independent multilayer profile: it explores what is associated with attraction, how desire is maintained or changes over time, and how intimacy, touch and bodily response are organised.

Not every important question belongs inside one endless test. P-120 therefore develops a separate system of optional research layers around the core profile. Each module examines its own domain and may exist independently of the others.

Not more questions in one test. More independent lenses.
Research system in development18+

The core profile remains unchanged

The Extended Research System does not “improve” or recalculate the P-120 result. It is a second research layer: a participant may choose one additional module, several of them, or none.

OPTIONAL RESEARCH LENSES

Five different questions — five different research branches

Each branch has its own construct boundary. It should not duplicate the core P-120 or a neighbouring module, and it does not create an overall Extended Total.

COM-12

How you talk about desire

Erotic Communication Architecture

Understanding one’s own desire is one thing. Expressing it so that another person can actually understand is another. COM studies preference expression, feedback, mismatch, negotiation and the ability to return to conversation after awkwardness or tension.

desire → expression → hearing the other → mismatch → negotiation → repair
In development · Research Candidate
MOT-12

Why you choose intimacy

Sexual Motivation Architecture

Two people may want the same thing while moving toward it for very different internal reasons. MOT studies not “what is arousing”, but why a person chooses erotically meaningful intimacy: which expected experiences, relational meanings and states stand behind the decision to enter contact.

not what I like → but why I choose this contact
In development · Research Candidate
SELF-12

How you experience your own sexuality

Sexual Self-Relation Architecture

Desire is not directed only outward. A person also experiences themselves: how easily desire can be acknowledged, whether pleasure feels legitimate, whether agency is retained, how self-consciousness is tolerated, and whether sexuality becomes a constant self-evaluation.

self-regard · desire legitimacy · agency · self-consciousness · shame
Candidate architecture · in development
LIFE-12/18

What happens beyond the intimate scene

Intimacy-Life Integration / Spillover

Intimacy does not exist in a vacuum. LIFE is being designed as an outcome/spillover layer that studies how intimate states coexist and change alongside emotional tone, subjective vitality, sleep, cognitive carryover, engagement in work or study, meaning and slower life anchors.

association / spillover — not causal destiny
Outcome research architecture · in development
RPE-MOD

Which specific form of experience fits you

Receptive / Sensory / Power / Fantasy-Enactment Architecture

A broad profile can show the overall architecture of arousal and bodily response. Real experience, however, may depend on much finer distinctions: intensity, sensory configuration, distribution of control, boundaries between fantasy and experience, and how much a person knows about their preferences from real situations.

RPE-MOD is being developed as an adaptive branch. The system should not ask everyone the same long list of sensitive questions. Relevant directions open only when they are actually applicable to the respondent.

preference ≠ tolerance ≠ capacity · fantasy ≠ experience
Adaptive research architecture · in development
CROSS-LAYER RESEARCH

The most interesting questions begin between layers

Each additional module may have research value on its own. But P-120 is designed as a system in which relationships between independent coordinates can become especially informative.

P-72 × MOT

What activates me — and why do I choose intimacy?

P-72D × COM

How does desire change over time — and can I communicate the need for change?

AO × MOT

How do regulation patterns coexist with motives for contact?

SOMA × SELF

What does the body notice — and how does the person evaluate their own bodily response?

RPE × COM

How precisely does a person know their preferences — and can they express them?

RPE × LIFE

Are fit or mismatch in specific preferences associated with downstream states?

Boundary. These relationships are research questions. They do not automatically become formulas, composite scores or causal conclusions without separate empirical authority.
ONE LEVEL DEEPER

And one level deeper — two people

An individual profile shows how one system is organised. Additional modules can examine it more precisely. But once data from two people are available, the question itself changes.

Not “how similar are they?” but “what happens when two architectures actually meet?”

SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARY

What this means — and what it does not yet mean

Extended modules do not change current P-120 scores.
There is no P-120 Extended Total.
There is no “intimacy index”.
There is no automatic compatibility score.
There is no rule that “more is better”.
Development completeness is not psychometric validation.
Research Candidate. This page presents the architecture of a developing research system. It does not claim that all future modules are already validated or available for completion.
